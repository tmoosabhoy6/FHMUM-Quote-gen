# Surface brief: the quote screen (index.html)

Mode: Operate. Visitor: FHMUM office staff at a desk, building and revising a quote several times a day.
Job: enter group size and flights, check what was worked out, choose stays and takhfeef, add charges, read the price, download Excel.
Constraints: one file, no build step; calculations, storage and Excel output untouched.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A quote is a journey with a price. The screen shows the journey as a band of days coloured by where the group sleeps, and keeps the price in view. It refuses the grey form with one accent colour, and the teal-and-star look it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: White libas and gold thread. Bright white sheets on a faint violet-grey wash, plum-black ink, one gold crochet band as the hem under the top bar, gold hairlines in the price sheet. Rida colours carry place: rose for Iraq, saffron for Cairo, mint for Saudi Arabia, lilac for time in the air. Figtree throughout, tabular figures for money. Boxed inputs, tick chips, ink-filled primary buttons.

STORY: Staff see the flights turn into coloured days, read each place's facts under its colour, and trust the price on the right because the working is visible. They download the Excel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with the mark, save status, Saved quotes, Settings and New quote, hemmed by the gold band. Left: one white sheet with the quote name, group size and exchange rates, then the flights table. Right: the sticky price sheet with per-za'ir prices and Download Excel. The journey band sits directly under the flights.

FORM: Standard two-column tool layout (form sheet plus sticky result sheet), position 1 on the list; the world supplies type, palette, density and the journey band only. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
